# SOLMAR — a Costa Crew story

An original sun-soaked open-world crime game, made for the [Make GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01). Rico Vale is a getaway driver for the Costa Crew, in a turf war against the Vulture Kings across the coastal city of SOLMAR.

## What is in it

- A living city: six north-south roads and four east-west roads, buildings with lit windows, palms, traffic and pedestrians, all grown from a seed.
- A 3D chase camera: the camera rides behind Rico and his car, low-poly city in perspective, golden-hour sky and sun, a police helicopter with a spotlight cone.
- Driving and stealing cars, a wanted level with pursuing police cars and AIR ONE, combat with ammo, health and armour.
- Eight linked missions from fixer Vera Solano — The Pickup, The Vela Job, The Bridge Job, Dockside Debt, Sunset Tow, Static on the Line, Harbor Lights and King Tide — ending in a win screen and free roam.
- A dense HUD: mission card with stage bar, police dispatch panel, weapon slots 1–7, money and wanted stars, rank and RP, crew cards (Dana Rook, Milo Ferran, Nia Sol), circular minimap, health and armour numbers, huge speed readout, radio ticker and dialogue with portraits.
- Saving and quick-save (F5), deterministic from a seed, static files only — no network.

## Controls

WASD drive and walk · E enter and exit cars · Mouse shoot · Z swap plates · X quick fix · C eyes out · F5 quick-save · Enter continue

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://127.0.0.1:8080/
```

Everything — the city, the characters, the vehicles, the story and the art — is original work made for this entry.
